Nearby is a calm, honest almanac of the wild things that share your patch of the world.

Each season, discover the birds, mammals, insects, fish, plants, spiders, snails, frogs and lizards that are genuinely active in your county: over 8,000 Swedish species, in your own language. Never "here right now", always the true season. No feeds to doom-scroll, no ads, no subscription. Just a quiet nudge now and then when something worth noticing is around.

• Spotted: build your county almanac as species come into season.
• Caught: head out to a nearby public place that suits the species (a lake for a pike, a meadow for a butterfly) and land it with a quick, one-thumb skill game.
• Helped: one-tap, honour-system ways to give back and protect, with the reason it matters.

Free to use, with 3 free catches every season. Unlock unlimited catching once with the Full Game, share it with up to 5 people with Family, and open other counties when you travel. Every species' facts and help-and-protect tips are always free.

Nearby respects you: no ads anywhere, and your position never leaves your phone. We only keep your home county. Export or delete your data anytime from Settings.
